    §2

    methods

    fig. 3

    The stack, charted as a sky. Each family of tools is a constellation and each ring is a year. A star rises the first year a role of mine used it and burns brighter the more roles used it, and the lines join each constellation in the order its stars appeared. Press play to watch the sky fill in.

    The same stack, set as a periodic table, the way it hangs on my github profile. Elements sit in their families; the column on the far right holds the noble gases, the inert ones that quietly run everything else. The two detached rows underneath are the tooling and the methods, and below them the radioactive series: the soft skills, unstable and best handled with care.

    And as a flow. Every thread is one tool used in one role: threads leave the roles on the left, gather by family in the middle and end at the tools on the right, so the thicker a tool, the more of my work has run through it.
